# Editions and Plans

[繁體中文](SUPPORT.zh-Hant.md) · [Guide](README.md)

| Feature | Tailwake (Basic) | Tailwake Pro |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Siri wake commands | Not included | Included |
| Shortcuts and scheduled automation | Not included | Included |
| Ads | Bottom banner when available | No ads |

Basic's manual wakes are free and unlimited. Tailwake Pro needs an active monthly, annual, or lifetime plan to send wakes.

## Tailwake Pro plans

- **Monthly** — US$1/month.
- **Annual** — US$10/year.
- **Lifetime** — US$30 one-time purchase.

These are US base prices. The app shows current pricing in your App Store storefront. Monthly and annual plans renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the period ends. A valid lifetime entitlement grants the same wake access without renewal. After expiration or revocation, Pro requires another valid entitlement before sending wakes.

Optional tips are **US$10** and **US$30**. Tips support development and leave wake access unchanged.

## Restore or redeem

In Tailwake Pro, open the info menu and choose **Plans**, or open the support screen. There you can choose **Restore Purchases** using the original Apple Account, **Redeem Offer Code** if you received an Apple offer code, or **Manage Subscriptions**. Apple handles billing and refunds. See the [Terms of Use](TERMS.md).
