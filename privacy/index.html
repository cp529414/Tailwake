Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 5, 2026

Tailwake home · 繁體中文

Summary

Tailwake stores your PC and relay configuration, wake history, and verification results on your device. Free users may see one Google AdMob banner on the main screen. Premium users, including valid lifetime owners, receive an ad-free experience. Tailwake does not require an account or operate a developer-run wake server.

Information on your device

PC names, addresses, MAC addresses, relay settings, and wake records are stored locally. SSH credentials and SecureOn passwords are protected in the iOS Keychain. Some limited Keychain information may remain after reinstalling. Device-only protected information is not synchronized to other devices. Tailwake does not supply these records or credentials as custom advertising targeting data.

Network activity

Local wakes send a Wake-on-LAN packet to your chosen network. Remote wakes connect to the SSH relay you configure. Verification contacts your chosen TCP address and port. These operational records are not sent to the developer or included in advertising requests as custom targeting data.

Google advertising and consent

Tailwake uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK for its free-user banner and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage applicable consent and privacy choices. Google may process IP addresses and approximate location inferred from them, device or app identifiers, crash and performance information, advertising data, and ad interactions to deliver, measure, secure, and improve advertising services. Google may use data for advertising measurement and analytics even though Tailwake does not add a separate analytics product. See Google's privacy policy and how Google uses information from apps.

Tailwake requests non-personalized advertising, disables Google's publisher first-party ID and optional device-location collection, and does not request access to the advertising identifier (IDFA) or Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission. Tailwake does not request Location permission for advertising. These choices do not mean that ads process no data: non-personalized ads can still use storage or identifiers for security, frequency limits, and measurement. Google can infer approximate location from an IP address even when optional location collection is off.

Where required, UMP presents consent or privacy choices before ad requests are allowed. Use Privacy choices in the app when available to review or change your choices, including applicable US state sale/sharing opt-outs. Declining consent does not remove manual wake functionality. An ad may be unavailable when consent, network conditions, or other requirements prevent it. Premium users do not load banner ads.

Purchases

Apple processes purchases and provides verified purchase and subscription status. Tailwake uses that status to grant automation and ad-free access. Tailwake does not receive your payment-card or banking details. Optional tips do not grant premium.

Permissions and controls

Local Network access allows local wake and verification operations. Notifications are optional and report wake outcomes. Change these permissions in iOS Settings. Google advertising consent and Apple's system permissions are separate controls. An advertising consent choice does not authorize cross-app tracking.

Deletion and questions

Deleting the app removes its local files; limited Keychain records may remain as described above. Google's processing is governed by its privacy information and applicable consent choices. For privacy questions or requests, use the developer contact details on Tailwake's App Store listing. Do not send SSH credentials or other secrets with a support request.

Changes

Tailwake asks you to review and accept material changes to its policy in the app before continuing.
