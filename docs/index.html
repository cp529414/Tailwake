Wake your PC from your iPhone.

Tailwake sends Wake-on-LAN magic packets from your iPhone or iPad, on your own network or through an SSH relay you control.

Read the instructions Compare Basic and Pro →
  • No Tailwake accountThere is no developer-run wake server. PC details stay on your device.
  • Your relay, your rulesUse a router or small Linux computer over SSH, with its host key pinned.
  • Verify and logSkip a wake when the PC is already online, and keep a log of every attempt.

How it works

Local (UDP)

On the same network, Tailwake broadcasts the magic packet directly. No relay is needed.

iPhone→PC

Relay (SSH)

Away from home, Tailwake signs in to an always-on relay on the PC's network over SSH, and the relay broadcasts the packet locally.

iPhone→Relay→PC

Verify and log

Tailwake can check whether the PC is online before and after waking, and keeps a Wake Log on your device.

Tailscale is one way to reach a relay privately without opening a router port.
