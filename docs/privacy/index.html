Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 24, 2026

Tailwake home

Summary

Both editions store PC and relay settings, wake history, and verification results on your device. Tailwake (Basic) may show up to two banner ads, from Google AdMob and Unity LevelPlay. Tailwake Pro is ad-free and includes no advertising or consent SDK. Neither edition requires a Tailwake account or uses a developer-operated wake server.

Information on your device

PC names, addresses, MAC addresses, relay settings, and wake records are stored locally. SSH credentials and SecureOn passwords are protected in the iOS Keychain. Some limited Keychain information may remain after reinstalling. Device-only protected information is not synchronized to other devices. Tailwake does not supply these records or credentials as custom advertising targeting data.

Network activity

Local wakes send a Wake-on-LAN packet to your chosen network. Remote wakes connect to the SSH relay you configure. Verification contacts your chosen TCP address and port. These operational records are not sent to the developer or included in advertising requests as custom targeting data.

Advertising and consent

Basic uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK and the Unity LevelPlay SDK for its banners, and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage applicable consent and privacy choices. Google and Unity may process IP addresses and approximate location inferred from them, device or app identifiers, crash and performance information, advertising data, and ad interactions to deliver, measure, secure, and improve advertising services. Both may use data for advertising measurement and analytics even though Tailwake does not add a separate analytics product. See Google's privacy policy, how Google uses information from apps, and Unity's privacy policy.

Basic asks for Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission before it starts Unity LevelPlay. If you allow tracking, Unity may use the advertising identifier (IDFA) and other device identifiers to show more relevant ads and measure them, including across other companies' apps and websites. If you decline, the advertising identifier is not available to Unity. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. Basic requests non-personalized advertising from Google, disables Google's publisher first-party ID and optional device-location collection, and Tailwake does not request Location permission for advertising. These choices do not mean that ads process no data: non-personalized ads can still use storage or identifiers for security, frequency limits, and measurement. Google can infer approximate location from an IP address even when optional location collection is off.

Where required, UMP presents consent or privacy choices before ad requests are allowed, and Unity LevelPlay receives that consent signal. Use Privacy choices in the app when available to review or change your choices, including applicable US state sale/sharing opt-outs; where Privacy choices are required, Tailwake also tells Unity not to sell or share your personal information. Declining consent does not remove manual wake functionality. An ad may be unavailable when consent, tracking choices, network conditions, or other requirements prevent it. Pro does not load banner ads.

Purchases

Apple processes Pro purchases and offer-code redemptions. Pro uses verified StoreKit entitlement status to enable manual wakes, Siri, and Shortcuts. Tailwake does not receive payment-card or banking details. Basic has no in-app purchases. Pro tips leave wake access unchanged.

Permissions and controls

Local Network access allows local wake and verification operations. Notifications are optional and report wake outcomes. Change these permissions in iOS Settings. Advertising consent, Apple's tracking permission, and Apple's other system permissions are separate controls. Allowing tracking is optional and does not change how wakes work.

Children

Tailwake is not directed to children under 13, or under the minimum age in your country or region, and does not knowingly collect personal information from them. Basic does not treat its ad requests as child-directed because the app is not child-directed.

Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal information, to object to its processing, to withdraw consent at any time, and to opt out of its sale or sharing. Tailwake has no account and no developer-operated server that holds your data, so the information described above stays on your device. For information held by Google or Unity, use Privacy choices in the app when available, the Tracking setting in iOS Settings, or the providers' own privacy controls (see Google's privacy policy and Unity's privacy policy). For anything else, use the developer contact details on Tailwake's App Store listing.

Deletion and questions

Deleting the app removes its local files; limited Keychain records may remain as described above. Google's and Unity's processing is governed by their own privacy information and applicable consent choices. For privacy questions or requests, use the developer contact details on Tailwake's App Store listing. Do not send SSH credentials or other secrets with a support request.

Changes

Tailwake asks you to review and accept material changes to its policy in the app before continuing.
