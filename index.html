Tailwake

Wake your PC from your iPhone or iPad, locally or through your own SSH relay.

Manual local and remote wakes are free and unlimited. Free users may see one bottom banner. Premium adds Siri, Apple Shortcuts, scheduled automation through Shortcuts, and an ad-free experience. Existing lifetime unlocks retain Premium benefits.
